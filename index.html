Our story

A neighborhood kitchen
with old-world roots.

Sage & Stone began as a tiny corner bistro with eight tables and one wood-fired oven. A decade later, we still bake our own bread, churn our own butter, and source nearly everything from farms within 60 miles.

We believe good food doesn't need to be complicated — it just needs to be made with care.

Elena Marchetti

Founder & Head Chef
